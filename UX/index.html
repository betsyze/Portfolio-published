Experience Design

I'm a full-stack design engineer: digital strategy, visual design, and, increasingly, web dev.

Expand context

I'm interested in weaving handmade-media into hardcore strategy and execution. I'm all about tech that's open source, scrappy, and heartfelt. My goal is twofold: to build practical tools that can evolve alongside users, and to never sacrifice my commitment to aesthetics.

At work, I've built complex design systems, navigated backend on internal utilities, and directed style guidance for UI + animated video. At home, I'm a self taught coder (read about this website), and artist (explore my art practice).
